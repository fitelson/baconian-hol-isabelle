   to have the same composition-agreement profile; and

of `fun'` propositions or the weaker \(Y\)-relative transport principle
implies weak L2.

immediately preceding split target.  The unsplit comparison targets are not
used as admitted lemmas.  The final combined target admits only the successful
collision-agreement, explicit-orbit-witness, and explicit-transport-witness

The complete finite base imports every axiom in

`$false` conjecture.  Thus the second-tier targets test the positive
consequences directly and contain no hidden denial of L2.

These timeouts establish no failure of derivability.  They show that the
staged search localizes Vampire's present obstruction exactly at the proposed
new PP consequences, rather than at the already checked algebraic bridges.
The composition-classification target is the primary one because it matches
the existing Isabelle reduction for L2.  The \(Y\)-relative transport target
is logically sufficient and weaker than full reversible-orbit reachability,
but Vampire did not prove it from the represented PP/D background.

Accordingly, the staged search does **not** establish
\(T_0+\mathrm{PP}+\exists\fun'\vdash\mathrm{L2}\).  It establishes three
precise sufficient conditions for L2 and isolates the still-unproved step as
the derivation of any one of those conditions from the represented
\(T_0+\mathrm{PP}+\exists\fun'\) background.

As with the monolithic THF file, every successful Vampire theorem is a result
about the represented higher-order fragment.  It must be replayed in Isabelle
before being counted as a new theorem of the full schematic CEV+ theory.
