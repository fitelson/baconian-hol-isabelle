# Results and corrections, 30 July 2026

## Invalid first run of target 20

A user-run Vampire 5.0.1 search on
`20_recombination_derives_fun_prime.tff.in` returned `SZS status Theorem` in
0.012 seconds. This is not a theorem of the intended Recombination-only
representation.

The proof exposed an encoding error in the first version of
`00_common_semantics.tff.in`. Proposition identity was represented by TFF
meta-level equality in `pointwise_eq_application` and `mf_prop_truth`.
Consequently, once unary Recombination obtained pointwise agreement at one
world, the agreement became true at every world automatically. Vampire could
then apply Modalized Functionality without zeroary Exhaustion. This erased
the exact modal gap established in Isabelle.

## Correction

The corrected common file now uses:

- `eq_prop_at(W,P,Q)` for proposition identity;
- `eq_op_at(W,X,Y)` for unary-operator identity;
- `eq_prop_term(P,Q)` for the proposition expressing proposition identity;
- an explicit rigidity axiom taking `eq_prop_at(W,P,Q)` to
  `box_true(W,eq_prop_term(P,Q))`;
- world-relative pointwise identity and Modalized Functionality.

The definitions of `fun_prime`, reversibility, sameness of kind,
composition-agreement profiles, orbit reachability, Y-relative transport, and
weak L2 have all been changed to use these object-language identity
relations.

The earlier Vampire proof applies only to the superseded encoding and must
not be cited as evidence that Recombination derives existence of a
`fun-prime` proposition.

No corrected file has yet been run by Codex.

## Corrected run of target 10

A user-run Vampire 5.0.1 search on the corrected
`10_full_QLN_derives_fun_prime.tff.in` returned `SZS status Theorem`.
The successful strategy found the refutation in 0.032 seconds; the portfolio
reported success in 0.141 seconds.

The proof uses the corrected world-relative identity vocabulary. Its
load-bearing input axioms are:

- truth of the proposition-identity term iff `eq_prop_at`;
- the pointwise-identity operator equation;
- the world-relative Modalized-Functionality condition;
- purity of the pointwise-identity operator and the
  Modalized-Functionality proposition;
- rigidity of proposition identity;
- Modalized Functionality;
- the definition of `fun_prime`;
- existence of a fundamental proposition;
- unary Recombination; and
- zeroary Exhaustion.

In particular, input axiom 57 in the Vampire proof is
`zeroary_exhaustion`. Thus the corrected proof does not reproduce the
invalid Recombination-only shortcut.

The proof does not use PP, zeroary Recombination, unary Exhaustion, uniqueness
of the fundamental proposition beyond its existence, or the no-higher-
fundamentals axioms. This strengthening is expected and agrees with the
Isabelle theorem
`CEV_exists_fun_prime_from_recombination_with_zeroary_exhaustion`: unary
Recombination, zeroary Exhaustion, and existence of a proposition-level
fundamental suffice for the represented bridge.

This run is a successful calibration of the corrected Vampire encoding. It
does not advance the unresolved derivation of L2.
